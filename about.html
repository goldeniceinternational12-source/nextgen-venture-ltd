What We Do

Our Core Services

Nine specialized finishing solutions, delivered by professionals who treat every project like a masterpiece.

01

Epoxy Floor Design

Seamless, glossy and ultra-durable epoxy flooring for homes, showrooms, warehouses and industrial spaces — resistant to stains, chemicals and heavy traffic.

02

POP Design

Artistic plaster-of-Paris ceilings, cornices, pillars and TV walls — from classic motifs to modern minimalist cove lighting designs.

03

Marble Trowel Paint (Sandtext)

Luxurious trowel-applied sandtext finishes that mimic natural marble texture — giving exterior and interior walls a rich, stone-like elegance.

04

Gravitex Paint

Weather-shield Gravitex coatings for long-lasting exterior protection — textured, crack-bridging and available in a wide range of colours.

05

Screeding

Precision floor and wall screeding for a perfectly level, smooth and ready-to-finish surface — the foundation of every great floor.

06

Painting

Interior and exterior professional painting with premium emulsion, satin and gloss finishes — crisp lines, even coats, stunning results.

07

Increte Stamp Floor

Decorative stamped concrete driveways, walkways and patios — patterned and coloured to replicate stone, brick or slate.

08

Tiling

Professional floor and wall tiling with precise layouts, clean finishes and durable installation for homes, offices, kitchens, bathrooms and commercial spaces.

09

Project Management

End-to-end supervision of your finishing works — scheduling, budgeting, quality control and handover, so you enjoy a stress-free build.
